One click, from your own browser

Set up once, on a computer with Chrome or Edge:

  1. Download the scanner, right-click the file, choose Extract All and extract it into your Documents folder. Leave it there.
  2. Type chrome://extensions in the address bar. Switch on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the sg-car-deals-scanner folder.

Then, each time you want fresh listings:

  1. Open your search on SGCarMart and set the filters you want.
  2. Click the green Scan for SG Car Deals button at the bottom right of that page. It reads the whole search, 100 cars at a time with a pause in between, up to 1,000 cars.
  3. Click Send to my dashboard. This page opens with the cars rated.

The scanner only reads the search results your browser is showing, and only sends them to this dashboard.

Share with your family

Listings you scan stay on this device until you publish them. Publishing saves them into your GitHub repository, so everyone who opens this dashboard sees them after about a minute.

Or add a saved page

On SGCarMart, open a used-car search, press Ctrl+S (Cmd+S on a Mac) and save the page. Then choose the saved .html file here.

How the rating works
  1. Depreciation. A used car's price is mostly prepaid depreciation: (price − what comes back as PARF when the COE ends) ÷ years of COE left. The app uses the figure SGCarMart shows.
  2. Fair depreciation. What that model and registration year normally depreciates at, from price guides read on 13 Nov 2026, scaled to the COE price you enter. When five or more similar cars are listed now, their median counts for half.
  3. Fair value and maximum price. Fair value = PARF value + fair depreciation × years left. Maximum price uses depreciation 5% under the norm.
  4. Score, 0 to 100. Price against fair value 30, depreciation 15, reliability 15, servicing and repairs 10, COE left 10 (1.5 to 3 years scores best), parts 5, PARF 5, mileage 5, and battery cover 5 for electric cars.
  5. Ratings. Exceptional: score 80+, at least 8% under fair value, a known model. Attractive: score 70+ and not above fair value. Overpriced: 8% or more above fair value. Avoid: a model flagged for reliability, or an electric car out of battery warranty.

Reliability, servicing, repair and insurance figures are estimates from the research, not measurements. A listing shows engine size but not power, so the COE category is an estimate for turbo cars near 1,600cc. Vans, trucks and buses are not rated. A good rating is a reason to view a car, not to buy it: check the service history, accident record and outstanding loan first.

Listings and figures come from sgcarmart.com; each car links to its page there. Personal tool, not affiliated with SGCarMart. Not financial advice.
